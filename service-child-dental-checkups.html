Child Dental Check-ups

Paediatric Dentistry in Thane West

Child dental check-ups in Thane West. Gentle six-monthly exams, cleaning and prevention for kids at Ratnagar Multispeciality Dental Clinic.

Book Your Appointment

What is child dental check-ups?

Regular check-ups let us spot cavities, bite problems and habits early. Our child-friendly approach keeps visits short, gentle and positive, so children grow up comfortable with dental care.

What problems does child dental check-ups solve?

  • Early cavities in milk and permanent teeth
  • Delayed or irregular tooth eruption
  • Crowding and early signs of bite problems
  • Plaque and gum issues in children
  • Parents' questions about brushing, diet and bottle habits

What to expect at Ratnagar Dental Clinic

  • Friendly introduction and examination
  • Cleaning and polishing where suitable
  • X-rays only when really needed
  • Simple tips for children and parents
  • Next visit reminder

Who should consider child dental check-ups?

Children from the eruption of the first tooth through the teenage years.

Frequently asked questions

How often should my child have a check-up?

Every six months for most children.

At what age can my child start using fluoride toothpaste?

A smear of fluoride toothpaste can be used from the first tooth. We guide you on the right amount.

Do you treat anxious children?

Yes. We go slowly and use gentle techniques to build trust.
